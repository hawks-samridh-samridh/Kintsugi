Kintsugi
Swift Student Challenge 2026

---

CONCEPT & INSPIRATION

Kintsugi (金継ぎ, "golden joinery") is the Japanese art of repairing broken pottery with gold lacquer. The philosophy holds that breakage and repair are part of the history of an object, not something to conceal. This app takes that philosophy and applies it to personal resilience — the idea that what breaks us also makes us more beautiful.

---

TECHNICAL

All geometry, physics, particle systems, and animations are generated procedurally in Swift/SceneKit.
No external assets, models, or third-party libraries were used.
No network calls are made.
